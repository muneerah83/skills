# Media Response — Farmer Training and Climate Resilience

Response paper prepared for a media enquiry received by the Department of Veterinary
Services (DVS) Malaysia on farmer training for climate change adaptation.

## The questions

Three principal questions with two supplementary parts:

1. Sustainability of closed-house poultry systems versus free-range/open farms —
   with a supplementary on whether the same approach would be mandated for livestock.
2. The DVS role in enabling field adoption of climate-resilient livestock genetics
   researched by MARDI, and the department's regulatory role — with a supplementary
   on collaboration with MARDI and with corporate producers.
3. How critical biological resilience and genetic climate adaptation are to
   safeguarding self-sufficiency levels toward 2030.

## Correction carried in the paper

The premise of Question 1 — that DVS mandated the phased closed-house rollout
starting in Perak — is not accurate. The requirement is a **Perak State Government**
policy, announced through the State Executive Councillor for Rural Development,
Plantations, Agriculture and Agro-based Industry, with full enforcement from
1 January 2027. Under the Ninth Schedule to the Federal Constitution, animal
husbandry and veterinary services sit on the Concurrent List, while land, local
government and planning are State matters. The DVS role is technical, animal-health
regulatory and advisory.

Section 3.0 of the paper sets out the correction and offers suggested wording.

A second, minor correction is carried at paragraph 4.11: MARDI is a federal agency
under the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security, not a state research body.
